id: kb-benign-histo
title: Recognising benign tissue on histopathology
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: histopathology
---
Benign tissue shows evenly spaced, uniform nuclei with regular architecture and no nuclear pleomorphism.
A benign tissue diagnosis on a small biopsy should be correlated with the clinical and radiological findings, and repeat sampling considered if there is discordance.
