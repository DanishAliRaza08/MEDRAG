title: Colorectal adenocarcinoma - histopathology
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: histopathology
---
Colorectal adenocarcinoma shows malignant glands with nuclear pleomorphism invading through the muscularis mucosae into the submucosa.
The pathology report should record tumour type, grade, depth of invasion, lymph node status, lymphovascular invasion and resection margins.
Mismatch repair protein immunohistochemistry should be performed on all colorectal carcinomas to screen for Lynch syndrome.
Benign colonic mucosa shows regular crypts lined by uniform nuclei without pleomorphism or invasion.
