title: The normal fundus
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: fundus
---
A normal fundus shows a pink optic disc with a cup-to-disc ratio below about 0.5, normal calibre vessels and a macula free of haemorrhages and exudates.
People with diabetes and a normal fundus should continue routine retinal screening.
