title: Invasive breast carcinoma - histopathology
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: histopathology
---
Invasive carcinoma of no special type is the most common histological type of breast cancer.
Histological features of invasive carcinoma include crowded malignant epithelial cells infiltrating stroma, nuclear pleomorphism and increased mitotic activity.
Breast carcinoma should be graded with the Nottingham system, which scores tubule formation, nuclear pleomorphism and mitotic count.
Oestrogen receptor, progesterone receptor and HER2 status should be determined on all invasive breast carcinomas to guide treatment.
All cases with suspected invasive carcinoma should be reviewed by a consultant pathologist and discussed at a multidisciplinary team meeting.
The differential diagnosis of an atypical epithelial proliferation includes ductal carcinoma in situ, sclerosing adenosis and radial scar.
