title: Pulmonary tuberculosis
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)

---
Pulmonary tuberculosis typically presents with chronic cough, fever, night sweats and weight loss.
Typical chest radiograph findings of post-primary tuberculosis are upper zone consolidation with cavitation and fibrosis.
Sputum samples should be sent for acid-fast bacilli smear, mycobacterial culture and a rapid molecular test such as Xpert MTB/RIF.
Tuberculosis should be considered in the differential diagnosis of upper zone consolidation, especially in high-prevalence settings.
Standard treatment of drug-susceptible tuberculosis is a six-month regimen of four drugs in the intensive phase followed by two drugs.
Suspected pulmonary tuberculosis should be notified to public health authorities and contacts should be traced.
