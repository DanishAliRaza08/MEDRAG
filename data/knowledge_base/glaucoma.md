title: Glaucomatous optic neuropathy
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: fundus
---
Glaucoma is a progressive optic neuropathy characterised by loss of retinal ganglion cells and visual field defects.
Optic disc cupping with an enlarged cup-to-disc ratio above about 0.6, or asymmetry between eyes of more than 0.2, is suspicious for glaucoma.
Raised intraocular pressure is the main modifiable risk factor for glaucoma.
Patients with suspected glaucomatous optic disc cupping should be referred for intraocular pressure measurement, gonioscopy, visual field testing and optical coherence tomography of the optic nerve.
The differential diagnosis of optic disc cupping includes physiological large cups and compressive optic neuropathy.
Treatment aims to lower intraocular pressure with topical medication, laser or surgery.
