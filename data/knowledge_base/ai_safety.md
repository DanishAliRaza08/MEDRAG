---
id: kb-ai-safety
title: Safe use of AI decision support in clinical reporting
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: general
---
AI-generated reports are decision support and must be reviewed and signed off by a qualified clinician before use in patient care.
Unsigned draft reports must be clearly marked as not for clinical use.
Clinicians should verify each cited source and remain accountable for the final diagnosis.
