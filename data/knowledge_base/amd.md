title: Age-related macular degeneration
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)

---
Age-related macular degeneration is a leading cause of central visual loss in older adults.
Drusen and pigmentary changes at the macula are characteristic fundus findings of early age-related macular degeneration.
Sudden distortion or loss of central vision suggests neovascular macular degeneration and should be referred urgently for anti-VEGF assessment.
