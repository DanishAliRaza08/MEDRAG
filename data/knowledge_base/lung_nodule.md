---
id: kb-nodule
title: Incidental pulmonary nodule
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: xray
---
A pulmonary nodule is a rounded opacity up to 30 millimetres in diameter that is surrounded by lung.
A nodule seen on a chest radiograph should be further characterised with computed tomography of the chest.
The risk of malignancy increases with nodule size, spiculated margins, upper lobe location, smoking history and older age.
Follow-up of solid nodules is based on size and risk, with surveillance CT or referral to a lung cancer multidisciplinary team.
