title: Systematic review of the chest radiograph
source: MedRAG sample corpus (educational summary)
modality: xray
---
A systematic approach to the chest radiograph reviews projection, airway, breathing, cardiac silhouette, diaphragm and soft tissues.
A normal chest radiograph shows clear lung fields, sharp costophrenic angles and a cardiothoracic ratio below 0.5.
A normal chest radiograph does not exclude early pneumonia, pulmonary embolism or small nodules, and clinical correlation is recommended.
